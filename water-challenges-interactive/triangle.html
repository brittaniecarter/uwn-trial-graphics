About the connections

Topics are grouped from the six slides in Water Challenges2 - Dominant Themes (1). A line joins two challenge areas when both slides mention a shared topic. Labels combine closely related wording; these are interpreted topic matches, not explicit links or measured relationship strengths supplied by the Director. Each topic counts once per pair, regardless of how often it appears. Select a connected topic to inspect the exact slide phrases.

The triangle groups the three categories around the selected challenge. Position and size do not represent quantities or measured causal relationships. Faculty respondent counts are retained from the October 7 faculty spreadsheet.
